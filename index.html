Sheet 02 / 06 — Approach Rev A

A brand is a set of decisions repeated consistently in space. A building is the same thing, built. I treat both as one continuous design problem, from the mark on a cup to the canopy over the pump island.

Trained as an architect, working as a graphic designer — I read drawings the way I read layouts, and I design layouts the way I read drawings.
